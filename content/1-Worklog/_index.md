The internship ran for 12 weeks, from **15/06/2026 to 04/09/2026**. The work
divides into three phases:

+ **Weeks 1–3 — Foundations and assessment.** AWS fundamentals, then a survey of
  what BorrowIt actually depended on in Supabase and what the AWS equivalent
  would be.
+ **Weeks 4–8 — Building the migration.** The four CDK stacks, in dependency
  order: network, database, containerised API, frontend.
+ **Weeks 9–12 — Hardening and documentation.** Observability, a high-availability
  demonstration, cost verification, and writing the workshop.
